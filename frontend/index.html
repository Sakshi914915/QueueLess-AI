GOOGLE GEMINI API

AI Intelligence That Respects Verified Data

QueueLess AI harnesses Google Gemini via secure backend endpoints to deliver instant answers in English and Hindi. Unlike generic bots, our assistant is strictly anchored to live, authorized MySQL records—preventing fabricated queue times or hallucinations.

  • Bilingual Customer Assistant: Answers questions about your active token, position, and wait times in fluent English and Hindi.
  • AI Executive Admin Analyst: Analyzes counter utilization, bottleneck services, and daily throughput for organizational managers.
  • Transparent Waiting Time Formula: Waiting times are calculated using mathematical queue theory, not unpredictable black-box models.
